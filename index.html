THE STORY

Saal 2089. ORION-7 ka crew aik khaas space mission par nikalta hai — sirf saat minute ka safar.

Magar jab unka raasta space-time ke andar mud jata hai, to woh saat minute unke liye unnees saal ban jaate hain. Zameen par sab kuch badal chuka hai.

Kya crew kabhi us duniya mein wapas laut sakega jo unhon ne peechhe chhodi thi?
